RearAware is still an experimental project and relies on real cat photos to train its AI model. While I have plenty of cat photos, only a small number contain visible cat butts. That's currently the biggest limitation of the model, and why detection is still hit and miss.

If you'd like to help improve RearAware, there are two ways to contribute:

  • Upload your own cat butt photos directly.
  • Let RearAware capture images during your video calls for you to review before uploading.
